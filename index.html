Glossary — How The Numbers Are Built

Plain-language definitions for the metrics used across this dashboard.

Period Goal (a.k.a. seasonal-weighted goal)

In one line: based on historical seasonality, this is how much should be booked by the end of the selected period — not the full-year goal, but the slice of it that history says lands by now.

Why it exists: the plan only gives an annual goal per producer. It does not say how much should land in any single month. Some months close more than others, so a flat “annual ÷ 12” would misjudge where we should be mid-year.

How it's built:

  1. For each prior complete year (2021–2025), measure the cumulative % of that year's won revenue that had landed by the end of each month — the seasonal pace curve.
  2. Average those curves across years to get a typical share-of-year-booked by each month-end.
  3. Multiply the annual plan by that cumulative share to get the Period Goal: Annual Plan × (cumulative seasonal share through the selected period).

Coverage %: Projected Finish (Booked + 90%+ Commit) ÷ Period Goal. Example: a $1.4M projected finish against a ~$2.4M June Period Goal is ~61% covered, leaving a ~$922K gap to fill from best-case (75%) deals, upside, or new activity.

Important caveat: this is a model-derived pace target, not a leadership-set monthly quota. If leadership has explicit monthly commit targets, treat those as the source of truth and override this number.

Forecast & Commit Terms

TermDefinition
BookedWon revenue already closed and recorded inside the selected window. Money in the bag.
Commit (90%+)Open deals at the 90%+ stage dated to close in the window — one push from closing. Counted at full value as the confident commit.
Projected FinishBooked + Commit (90%+). The realistic expected landing point for the window.
Best Case UpsideProjected Finish plus 75%-stage deals at best case. The optimistic ceiling if late-stage deals all land.
WindowThe forecast period in focus: This Month, This Quarter, or Next Month.
SlippedAn open deal whose predicted close date is already in the past — needs re-dating or a status update.

Pipeline & Revenue Terms

TermDefinition
Won RevenueClosed recurring revenue credited to a producer in the period.
Total ContributionReferral / convergence (cross-practice) sales only — measured against the Convergence Goal Tracker. This is the credited cross-sell contribution and does not include a producer's primary-practice won revenue. Florida is tracked as two markets: North/Central Florida and South Florida. Because convergence tracking began in 2026, there is no prior-year history, so no seasonal pace or “behind pace” comparison is shown for this metric — only attainment against the annual goal.
Convergence RevRevenue from cross-practice / cross-sell (referral) deals (e.g., an EB producer sourcing a P&C opportunity). This is the basis for Total Contribution and is compared to each market's goal in the Convergence Goal Tracker.
Raw PipelineTotal face value of open deals, regardless of stage probability.
Weighted PipelineOpen deal value × the stage probability (25%=0.25, 50%=0.50, 75%=0.75, 90%+=0.92). A risk-adjusted view.
StageDeal progression: 25% (early), 50% (qualified), 75% (late), 90%+ (near close).
OverdueAn open deal whose predicted close month is before the current month — past due and needs attention.
Delinquent PipelinePipeline value flagged as stale / aged past its expected activity window (from producer data).
ProducerAnyone with a revenue goal assigned in the plan.
Behind PaceA producer whose booked-to-date trails the seasonal pace curve for where they should be by now.
